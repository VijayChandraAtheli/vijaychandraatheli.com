  ### Vijay Chandra Atheli's

  ## Pragmatic Perspectives on Strategy, Analytics, and Salesforce

  This journal reflects how I think about modern businesses, and how the intersection of strategy and analytics shapes how companies operate, scale, and compete.

  Rather than treating business strategy, data, analytics, and CRM as isolated disciplines, the writing here explores how their interaction drives real outcomes. Decisions leaders trust or question. Systems that scale or quietly erode. Insights that compound value over time, or introduce risk.

  Grounded in real-world consulting across analytics, data platforms, and Salesforce-based CRM systems, the focus is practical, systems-level thinking that connects intent to execution and analysis to action.
